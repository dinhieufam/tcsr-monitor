\section*{S4. External-Audit Caveat}

A cached CholecSeg8k audit used 8080 frames, but the current external
prediction/label pipeline produced failures for every frame at $\tau=0.5$,
$0.6$, and $0.75$ (mean IoU $0.128$). AUROC and AUPRC are therefore
unidentifiable on that audit, so it is not reported as external validation.
